# Welcome to my program, Duvo Eins!

## What is Duvo Eins?
Duvo Eins is a learning program made with Python/PyGame. Two players can play this game, and each will have to solve math questions (K-8) to attack the other person's avatar, which is customized.

## How do I install Duvo Eins?

### Version 2P.0.5.0 (August 2026)
The first verison out of release, built in August 2026, can be installed in two ways... by downloading the .exe version or you can downlaod the folder "version2P.0.50" from the Duvo Eins GitHub Repository.

## How do I use Duvo Eins? (version2P.0.5.0)
You can open the .exe verison or run the files from the repository in a code editor. Once you enter, you will be greeted with the menu screen!

### Information:
Press the INFO button on the menu screen to get the information. You will see the version as well as some instructions. The instructions are also pasted below:

1. Enter names on Selection Screen 1.
2. Triple-tap 1-4 to pick a player.
3. Answer math questions by typing 1-4 (p1) or 7-0 (p2) to attack.
4. Press TAB to go back / ESC to quit

### Settings:
You can press the circular button with the settings icon on the menu screen to access the settings screen. Here you can change the grade levels for each player. Keep in mind the backup library is smaller (in case the .json database can't be detected), so only grades K & 1 are available. If a grade is not available, K becomes the level.

### Selection:
Once you press the START button on the menu screen, selection has begun! Type Player 1's name and press enter. Then type Player 2's name and press enter to go to the second part of selection. If both names are the same, you will be stuck on the first selection screen until the names are different.

For the second selection screen, Player 1 can tap each key (1-4, corresponding keys are shown) to check the stats of each color. Each color has a color is it strong to (1.2x damage given & 0.8x damage taken) and a color it is weak to (0.8x damage given & 1.2x damage taken). You can use this to even out playing when a higher-skilled player plays against a lower-skilled player. Then, Player 1 can triple-tap a key to select the character. Afterwards, Player 2 does the same process. Both can have the same color, but it is preferred to have different colors for distinguishability. 

### Gameplay:

#### Player 1:
A question will pop up from your chosen grade level (K being the default). After figuring out your answer, press the key corresponding to your choice (first- 1, second- 2, third- 3, fourth- 4). Then, choose between attacking the other player (base damage 10) by pressing the A key or defending by adding a shield (stopping 1 attack each) by pressing the D key.

#### Player 2:
A question will pop up from your chosen grade level (K being the default). After figuring out your answer, press the key corresponding to your choice (first- 7, second- 8, third- 9, fourth- 0). Then, choose between attacking the other player (base damage 10) by pressing the J key or defending by adding a shield (stopping 1 attack each) by pressing the L key.

### Endgame:
Once someone's health goes to 0, the endgame screen will popup, showing the winner plus some basic stats. By pressing TAB, you can go back to the menu screen. By pressing ESC, you can quit the program! That is all!
